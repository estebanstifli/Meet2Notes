# Roadmap

## Completed: local meeting intelligence foundation

- Native microphone, audio-interface, and available system-audio capture.
- Real-time Faster Whisper transcription with a final quality pass.
- Runtime-aware CPU/CUDA profiles and independently resident AI workers.
- sherpa-onnx speaker diarization and transcript speaker assignment.
- LFM2.5 1.2B Q4_K_M local summaries through llama.cpp.
- Friendly Settings tabs for transcription, diarization, and summary engines.
- One-command installation and managed model download/verification.
- Editable, persistent transcription versions and timestamped segments.

## Next: notes and exports

- Editable speaker names and merge/split speaker tools.
- Structured decisions, actions, dates, risks, and unresolved questions.
- TXT, Markdown, JSON, SRT, and VTT exporters.
- Summary templates for stand-ups, interviews, lessons, and sales calls.

## Later

- Additional local and opt-in remote transcription/summary providers.
- Native macOS Core Audio Tap support for desktop audio.
- Local semantic search and meeting chat.
- Signed desktop installers and automatic application updates.
