Privacy by architecture

Your conversations are not the product.

Meet2Notes binds locally, stores recordings in your private workspace and enables no telemetry or third-party network service by default.

  • ✓ Local recording and native inference
  • ✓ Explicit controls before data can leave
  • ✓ Open source and inspectable
